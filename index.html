      Gross WPM = (characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ active minutes

      Net WPM = Gross WPM − (mistakes ÷ 5 ÷ active minutes)

      Accuracy = (correct characters ÷ characters typed) × 100

      5 characters = 1 word. 5 mistakes reduce the speed by 1 word.
      Characters you have not reached are not mistakes. Pause time is not counted.
