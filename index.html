Test result

    

    

      Gross WPM = (characters typed ÷ 5) ÷ active minutes

      Net WPM = Gross WPM − (wrong words ÷ active minutes)

      Accuracy = (correct printed words ÷ printed words typed) × 100

      The result is checked the way a printed page is checked. Extra spaces are ignored.
      A word is wrong only when the printed spelling does not match. One wrong word reduces the speed by 1 word.
      Words you have not reached are not mistakes. Pause time is not counted.
      Qualifying speed for this Anukampa Niyukti test: 28 net WPM.
